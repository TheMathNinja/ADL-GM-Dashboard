About the model

Cutoffs are the mean simulated midpoint APW between ranks 15/16 for a win and 17/18 for a tie. Points For breaks APW ties, followed by Potential Points in both leagues. Earlier outlooks use the selected week’s information with today’s corrected scores.

Weekly Potential Points are sampled as PF plus a random nonnegative gap conditioned on PF, the observed team gap, and NFL bye count, using separate league history. Potential is the third tiebreaker. This historical fit retains some downward Potential bias.
