How standings and predictions work

Each race ranks teams by accumulated All-Play wins (score ties count as ½), then points scored. FAFL also uses potential points as its next tiebreaker. Ranks 1–15 earn a win, 16–17 a tie, and 18–32 a loss. Completed races show their actual outcome.

ADL predicts future scoring from season-to-date PPG and Potential PPG, trained on completed seasons beginning in 2018. Each simulation includes one uncertain team-strength estimate that persists across future weeks, plus independent normal weekly scoring variation.

FAFL keeps its current playoff engine’s projected PPG, weekly scoring variability and freshly calculated week-specific team-strength uncertainty. Independent weekly scoring errors follow a Student-t distribution with 5 degrees of freedom, scaled to preserve the same variance. This allows occasional extreme scoring weeks.

Cutoffs are the mean across simulations of the midpoint in APW between ranks 15/16 (win) and 17/18 (tie). Completed races show actual midpoint cutoffs. A cutoff alone does not resolve an APW tie. All-Season is a separate fifth Bonus Game spanning Weeks 1–12.

Earlier outlooks use only scores through the selected week, with today’s corrected score history. They are reconstructed forecasts, not archives of the originally published pre-correction predictions. No preseason Q1 forecast is available. Unstarted quarters share the same outlook because these models assume the same future scoring process in each quarter.
