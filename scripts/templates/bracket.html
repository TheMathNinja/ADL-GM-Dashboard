Playoff Rules

Playoff Qualification and Seeding

Each of a conference's 4 division winners will earn an automatic bid into the ADL playoffs, with winners determined by the following order of criteria:

  1. Overall Winning Percentage
  2. Head-to-Head Record*
  3. All-Play Record
  4. Total Points Scored
  5. Potential Points

* Defined as a team’s overall record if played in a “mini-league” of only the tied teams

In addition, three Wild Card teams from each conference will advance to the ADL playoffs, selected with the following order of criteria:

  1. Overall Winning Percentage
  2. All-Play Record
  3. Total Points Scored
  4. Potential Points

Once playoff teams have been chosen, they are seeded within their conference's playoffs (and are re-seeded every round during the playoffs to determine matchups) by the following order of criteria:

  1. Cumulative All-Play Record
  2. Total Points Scored
  3. Potential Points

Whether a team is a division winner or has a higher winning percentage than another will not affect playoff seeding. This minimizes division strength and maximizes overall team performance as our seeding criteria. In the most extreme case, this makes it possible for the #1-4 seeds to all hail from the same division, and for seeds #5-7 to all be distinct division winners.

Playoff Format

The overall ADL playoff structure follows the NFL, where the top seed receives a bye in the first round, and each round’s matchups are determined by pairing the strongest team with the weakest team (according to the latest seed rankings). Every team will have matchups through Week 17 (even if they are eliminated), according to the following schedule:

Weeks 13-14: Wild Card Round Doubleheader games

Week 15: Divisional Round games + 1st Leg of Conference 5th Place Game

Week 16: Conference Championships + 2nd Leg of Conference 5th Place Game + Conference 3rd Place Game

Week 17: Super Bowl + Bragging Rights Games (2nd vs. 2nd through 7th vs. 7th placed teams between AFC and NFC)

The Wild Card Round spans two weeks to mitigate the impact of Bye Weeks on elimination. Each playoff franchise’s Wild Card Round score will be determined by summing their Week 13 and Week 14 scores.

In the event of a tie in a playoff (or consolation ladder) game, the team with the higher seed will be declared the winner (consider this home-field advantage).

The Conference 5th Place Game is a two-week matchup among the 3 Wild Card Round losers. The winner and runner-up are determined by cumulative points scored over Weeks 15 and 16. Games including three franchises (this game and Consolation Ladder Game 4) will display as head-to-head matchups between the top two seeds on MFL but are true three-team games.

Consolation Ladder Format

Every ADL franchise that fails to qualify for their conference's playoff will continue to compete in their conference’s postseason Consolation Ladder. Like playoff teams, teams within the consolation ladder will be initially seeded by the following order of criteria:

  1. All-Play Record
  2. Total Points Scored

For weeks 13-17, there will be 4 ranked matchups in each conference:

Consolation Game #1: #8 Seed vs. #9 Seed

Consolation Game #2: #10 Seed vs. #11 Seed

Consolation Game #3: #12 Seed vs. #13 Seed

Consolation Game #4: #14 Seed vs. #15 Seed vs. #16 Seed

The Consolation Ladder features no double-headers; each round spans a single week. Every week, the team that wins its matchup (achieves the highest score in the case of Game #4) will be promoted to its conference's next-highest consolation game, and the losing team will be demoted to the next-lowest game, with the exception that the winner of Game #2 may only advance to Game #1 if they outscore the loser of Game #1 that week. In the event of a repeat match-up in Game #1, the game becomes a “Best-Of Series” with series ties broken by cumulative points scored. The teams that win Game #1 and lose Game #5 will remain in their respective games the next week. The team that wins Game #1 in Week 17 will be declared its conference's Consolation Ladder Champion.

About the probabilities

Single-game probabilities preserve the 400-point Elo logistic formula and multi-team softmax. For summed-score rounds, independent equal-scale Gumbel performances extend that model across both legs. A completed first-leg points lead shifts the remaining logistic probability using a league-specific historical points scale. Current Elo is held fixed across future legs.

Consecutive repeats in Consolation Game 1 are decided by series W-L, then cumulative points. Actual tied games use the higher seed.
