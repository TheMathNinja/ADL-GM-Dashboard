# Live Bonus outcome probabilities

W/T/L Prob is the chance of occupying a Bonus Win / Tie / Loss position **at the end of the current week**, matching the Live view's Projected End of Week scope. It is not a forecast of unplayed later weeks. The current quarter and Regular Season carry forward their own completed-week APW/PF/Potential totals and are ranked separately in every draw.

The same 20,000 joint final-week scoring draws supply both H2H and Bonus probabilities. Simulated weekly points are rounded to league 0.1-point precision, used to recompute all 32 teams' weekly all-play records, and added to prior results. Ranking uses APW then cumulative PF. Where an entire tied group's eligible NFL rosters have finished, recorded Potential is the third key. If future Potential could still break an exact APW/PF tie, the tied group splits the available result slots proportionally; no franchise-ID fallback or fabricated Potential projection is used. This is an approximation for rare unresolved Potential tiebreakers.

Each draw allocates 15 Bonus Win slots, 2 Tie slots, and 15 Loss slots. Ties in All-Play count as half wins. Each team's three probabilities sum to one. Displayed whole percentages use largest-remainder rounding so they sum to 100%; sorting uses unrounded expected Bonus wins (win plus half tie). Current/Projected toggles show the same probabilities because both are conditioned on actual scores and remaining player time.

All live Bonus probabilities are included in the existing per-scrape archive for later calibration checks. Full-game scoring checks do not establish minute-level Bonus probability calibration.
