# Live final Bonus outcome probabilities

W/T/L Prob gives the probabilities of the **final current-quarter and Regular Season Bonus Win / Tie / Loss results**, including every later week through each event's conclusion. The standings themselves still show Current or Projected End of Week standings; the probabilities forecast the final event.

The current week's 20,000 joint player-score/clock draws are combined with the deployed shared Bonus Games/Playoffs engine's later-week PF/Potential trajectories. ADL uses the selected 35/0/55/10 EB/NIG engine; FAFL uses its deployed F315 engine. The official shared forecast is called once per worker/checkpoint and cached. Its current-week draw is replaced by the live draw, so it is never counted twice. Its later-week persistent uncertainty and paired Potential draws are retained. Future team strength is held at the deployed prior-week forecast; live scores update this week's outcome, without an unvalidated live refit of future ability.

Actual completed weeks are carried forward separately for the quarter and Reg Season. Each draw recomputes all-play standings through the relevant final week, then ranks by APW and cumulative PF. Recorded Potential and future paired Potential serve as the third key once the current eligible roster's NFL games are finished. When unfinished current-week Potential could break an exact APW/PF tie, the tied group splits the available result slots proportionally rather than inventing deterministic Potential. This is an approximation for rare unresolved Potential tiebreakers.

Each draw allocates 15 Bonus Win slots, 2 Tie slots, and 15 Loss slots. The three probabilities sum to one per team. Whole-percent display uses largest-remainder rounding to total 100%; sorting uses unrounded expected Bonus wins (win plus half tie). Current/Projected toggles show the same final-event probabilities.

All probabilities are archived per scrape, along with the deployed future-model version, source commit, checkpoint, simulation count, and hash of the paired later-week trajectories for replay. Dependencies match the deployed scoring engine. Missing/stale official forecast inputs or model validation failures produce unavailable odds; end-of-week or H2H odds are never substituted. Preseason forecasts remain unavailable until the deployed engine supports that checkpoint. These live conditional probabilities have not yet been validated against archived minute-level outcomes.
