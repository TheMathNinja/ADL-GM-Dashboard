# ADL shared player-based EB / NIG model

The selected scoring input is 35% availability-adjusted Potential, 0% adjusted PPG, 55% projected Starter Strength and 10% projected Roster Strength. This mean and its matching covariance-constrained EB posterior supply one PF/Potential simulation for H2H, All-Play, playoff odds and all five Bonus Games. NFL target-week byes and known absences enter the player inputs once. The observed league YTD PPG baseline and previously fitted 2018+ NIG physical innovation model are retained.

R05/L17 player forecasts use position-specific, equal-player-season nonnegative affine Week 1 public Fantasy Sharks priors, changing ability updated by actual scores, flat-start/current-season positional opponent adjustments and Beta-Bernoulli participation. Later weekly Sharks projections are not used. Player priors are trained on Week-1-rostered or 7-of-12 rostered players; inference includes every owned player. Missing player inputs use the same documented position-prior/observed-score fallback as research. Player fits for 2026 use completed 2021–2025; team input fits use 2022–2025, with player team inputs generated from earlier-season fits. Prior-season scale conversion gives the aggregate inputs comparable between-team spreads before the fixed percentages are applied.

Starter Strength selects a legal expected-score lineup, not the best realized lineup. Roster Strength sums all owned players, including depth/reserves; reserve/taxi players cannot enter the starting lineup without activation. These inputs describe each remaining regular-season week separately.

The public Roster Strength rating deliberately uses the separate 0/0/90/10 input recipe and its own fitted EB reliability. Rating = 100 × that model's mean remaining-regular-season scoring forecast / league YTD PPG. League average is exactly 100 before display rounding; rank is league-wide, from unrounded ratings. This index is not displayed in PPG units and does not replace the selected 35/0/55/10 scoring forecast.

Every preliminary and correction refresh fetches current fantasy rosters directly from MFL, refreshes public NFL player scores/status evidence, reconstructs player posteriors and legal lineups, and writes common player-team outlooks. Current ownership can therefore affect forecasts before a new game is played. One current roster is held fixed over future weeks. Earlier published outlooks retain their original ownership/forecast snapshots. Failed roster or strength refresh stops the forecast path rather than publishing stale inputs.

Annual prior artifacts are versioned and fail closed when absent. Model choices remain exploratory selections from 2023–2025, not independent future confirmation. Weekly refreshes update observations; they do not optimize the selected percentages again.
