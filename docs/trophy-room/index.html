How these records are calculated

Careers follow individual GMs across franchises using the same audited history and name aliases as Playoff Picture. GMs who have always co-owned together share one row, with their results counted once. GMs with any separate ownership stint retain separate rows. Selecting a GM shows the seasons included.

All-play percentage = (wins + ½ ties) ÷ comparisons. RS includes Weeks 1–12; full-season includes Weeks 1–16 through 2020 and Weeks 1–17 from 2021. Every team is compared with all 31 others each week, including bye weeks. All-Time All-Play% is the average of each completed season’s full-season all-play percentage. Every year receives equal weight, including playoff weeks.

Record is the finalized league W-L-T total, including bonus games and postseason results. Historical bonus formats are preserved as recorded in the season standings. Completed seasons end in 2025; Playoff Picture adds the current season’s completed weeks to this same career baseline. Ranks here are among the selected owner pool, with ties sharing rank. Current status uses the 2026 GM roster.
