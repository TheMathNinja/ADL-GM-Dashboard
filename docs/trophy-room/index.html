How these performances are ranked

Regular season means Weeks 1–12. Playoff lists include scheduled games by that season’s playoff field, including placement games and the Super Bowl. Byes and consolation teams are excluded. Offensive Points sum QB, RB, WR and TE plus PK, PN and historical KR points. Defensive Points sum DT, DE, LB, CB and S points. Individual player lists cover all NFL player games, including bench players and free agents; duplicate fantasy copies count once.

Higher points rank first. Ties share rank. Team lists include ties at tenth; individual lists include ties at twenty-fifth. Native team totals preserve MFL’s official stored result; a popup identifies any difference from the listed player sum. Current Scoring keeps original starters and positional eligibility, applies the 2026 reconstructed player-score overlay, and reoptimizes Potential Points under that season’s lineup limits. Missing-data games and historical positions with no 2026 scoring equivalent are excluded rather than assigned zero. Supplemental stat lines come from NFL/nflverse data when available. How seasonlong scores are ranked

Team lists show the top 10; individual lists show the top 25. Ties at the cutoff are included. Higher totals or winning percentages rank first. Ties count as half a win. Regular Season covers Weeks 1–12. Team Playoffs includes scheduled games by the actual playoff field, including placement games and the Super Bowl; byes and consolation teams are excluded. Combined includes regular season plus eligible playoff games. Individual lists include all NFL player games in the selected weeks, independent of fantasy starts.

All-play compares each team with all 31 league opponents in every included week. Team Record includes bonus games for Regular Season and Combined. Native bonus records follow the same audited MFL totals as Season Archive. Current Scoring recalculates scheduled outcomes, all-play, and the same bonus-game format used that year. Bonus ranking uses league-wide all-play wins, then points and potential points. Four quarterly awards apply through 2020, with a regular-season award from 2021. Through 2024, each conference awards seven wins, two ties, and seven losses; from 2025, the league awards fifteen wins, two ties, and fifteen losses. Both leagues use the same five-bonus format from 2021. FAFL’s 2023 published records contain a separate adjustment matching Q4: Native Scoring retains and labels it as an MFL adjustment; Current Scoring does not invent a sixth bonus game.

Current Scoring uses the existing reconstructed 2026 scoring overlay for earlier years and recorded MFL scores for the current season, preserving original starters and historical lineup limits. Potential Points reoptimizes legal lineups. Incomplete reconstructed season totals and unsupported historical positions are excluded. The current season is explicitly marked partial. Open any performance to see its weekly results and original MFL scoring links.

How these records are calculated

Careers follow individual GMs across franchises using the same audited history and name aliases as Playoff Picture. GMs who have always co-owned together share one row, with their results counted once. GMs with any separate ownership stint retain separate rows. Selecting a GM shows the seasons included.

All-play percentage = (wins + ½ ties) ÷ comparisons. RS includes Weeks 1–12; full-season includes Weeks 1–16 through 2020 and Weeks 1–17 from 2021. Every team is compared with all 31 others each week, including bye weeks. All-Time All-Play% is the average of each completed season’s full-season all-play percentage. Every year receives equal weight, including playoff weeks.

Record is the finalized league W-L-T total, including bonus games and postseason results. Historical bonus formats are preserved as recorded in the season standings. Completed seasons end in 2025; Playoff Picture adds the current season’s completed weeks to this same career baseline. Ranks here are among the selected owner pool, with ties sharing rank. Current status uses the 2026 GM roster.
