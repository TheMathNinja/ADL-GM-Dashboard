Conference awards

How awards are determined

Head Coach goes to the conference leader in all-play wins against all 31 teams. Ties count as half a win. Offensive Coordinator, Defensive Coordinator and General Manager go to the conference leaders in offensive, defensive and potential points, respectively. Each period uses only its completed weeks once the period is final. Tied winners share their conference’s cash prize.
