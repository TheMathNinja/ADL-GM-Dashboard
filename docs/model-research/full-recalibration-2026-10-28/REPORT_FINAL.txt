Limitations:
Only five seasons for all-other-seasons validation and three for chronological validation; overlapping weekly outlooks are not independent seasons.
2021 MFL injury snapshots are empty; 2021 status credits remain zero/raw Potential, with original archived active-score metadata retained. Injury/bye credit status labels come only from the saved weekly MFL reports.
Historical weekly injury/roster snapshots are retrospective and not all verified as exact pre-kickoff captures.
Qualification simulation applies current rules, including all five Bonus Games, to each historical score stream. It matches audited historical qualifiers in 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2025; the 2024 CIN/CLE places differ. Both current-rules replay and actual historical-field Brier errors are saved.
Point metrics use equal season/checkpoint/team weights; weekly-score metrics average remaining weekly errors within each checkpoint.
The earlier broadened-cohort and average-PPG-objective experiments are separate artifacts and are not this final result.
Public Elo playoff-bracket probabilities and FAFL models were not changed.
